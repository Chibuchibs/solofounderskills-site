A game literally nobody could beat

The instruction said "tap the side you're falling towards". The code pushed you in the direction you tapped — so it was telling players to speed up their own fall. Over 300 simulated runs:

What you doTimes you fall

Following the instructions was worth exactly as much as not playing. No difficulty tweak would ever have fixed it.
